Overview

The seven colors of the rainbow are red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. These colors are considered some of the most historic and famous colors.

Details

Sir Isaac Newton split white light through a glass prism in the early 1700s. What he found was fascinating, he saw five main colors. Newton added orange and indigo to create a group of 7 colors, matching a music scale. However, a rainbow is a blend of millions of colors, rather than a strict 7 lines.

Indigo Debate


There is a major debate regarding Indigo's validity in the "big seven" of the rainbow.

Critics of Indigo argue that it is too similar to blue and violet, and it is not deserving of the distinguishment of being a color. Many people believe that indigo is a variation of blue or violet, and that Newton only included seven colors because of other concepts such as days of the week and planets.

Rainbow Representation


Rainbows are used to represent many things in modern culture.

For example, LGBTQ+ identifying people have used the rainbow-like Pride flag to symbolize diversity and inclusvity. The "Rainbow Nation" was a term used to describe post-apartheid South Africa, celebrating multiculturalism. People also believe a rainbow appearing after a storm represents good things to come.
